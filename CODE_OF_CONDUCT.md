# Community conduct

Gyro contributors and maintainers should keep project discussions respectful and useful. Critique code and decisions without attacking people. Give others room to ask questions, disagree, and learn.

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualized comments, repeated personal attacks, and publishing someone's private information are not accepted. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project-managed spaces.

If you experience or observe a problem, email [kavrindev@gmail.com](mailto:kavrindev@gmail.com) with the subject `Gyro API community conduct`. Do not publish private evidence in an issue.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary. They will consider the behavior, context, and safety of the people involved. Reports should be shared only with the people needed to handle them, subject to legal obligations.
